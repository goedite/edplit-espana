FAQ
What are diamond tools?
These are cutting, drilling or grinding tools with a diamond coating or working segments. Diamonds are the hardest material that effectively processes reinforced concrete, stone, ceramics, asphalt and other durable materials.

What is the lifespan of professional diamond tools?
Durability depends on the tool class and the processed material. Professional DISTAR blades of the 3D series provide 15–25 m² of concrete cutting, the 5D series – 30–50 m², the 7D series – up to 100 m² or more. With proper use, one blade replaces dozens of conventional abrasives.

Are diamond tools suitable for home use?
Yes, diamond tools are universal and suitable for both professional and home use. For DIY users, DISTAR offers the 3D Bestseller series – an optimal price-quality ratio for occasional use.

Where are diamond tools most commonly used?
Main areas of application: construction (cutting concrete, brick, wall grooving), stone processing (production of countertops, monuments), tiling works (cutting porcelain stoneware), road construction (cutting joints in asphalt), industry (processing reinforced concrete prefabricates). Diamond tools are indispensable wherever precise and fast processing of hard materials is required.
